# Quickstart Guide: 008-business-profile

## Validation Scenario 1: Setup Store Details & Logo

1. Open the app and navigate to **الإعدادات** (Settings).
2. Tap on the new section: **بيانات المنشأة وترويسة الفواتير**.
3. Enter:
   - Store Name: `مؤسسة النيل لمهمات السباكة الحديثة`
   - Owner Name: `الحاج محمود عبد الله`
   - Primary Phone: `01098765432`
   - Secondary Phone: `01234567890`
   - Address: `شارع البحر، المحلة الكبرى`
   - Commercial Register: `12456/7`
   - Tax Card: `456-789-123`
4. Tap **اختيار الشعار** and pick an image from local gallery.
5. Verify the live preview card at the top reflects the Arabic store header with the logo.
6. Tap **حفظ التغييرات**.

---

## Validation Scenario 2: Verify PDF Quotation Header

1. Navigate to **عروض الأسعار** (Quotations).
2. Open any quotation and tap **مشاركة PDF / طباعة**.
3. Verify:
   - Header displays the store name `مؤسسة النيل لمهمات السباكة الحديثة`.
   - Contact numbers appear formatted with LTR digits.
   - Store logo appears at top right.
   - Any configured default quotation validity terms appear in the footer notes.

---

## Validation Scenario 3: Verify PDF Invoice Legal Credentials

1. Navigate to **فواتير المبيعات** (Invoices).
2. Open any invoice and tap **طباعة / تصدير PDF**.
3. Verify:
   - Commercial Register and Tax Card numbers are printed clearly below the store name.
   - Return & exchange policy terms are printed at the bottom note box.
